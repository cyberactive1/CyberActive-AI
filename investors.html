Building the Autonomous Security Layer for the AI Infrastructure Economy.

As AI infrastructure expands, the complexity and consequence of security incidents increase. CyberActive AI is developing patented autonomous security and resilience technology for AI workloads, GPU compute, and the infrastructure that connects them.

Investment thesis

Why the Opportunity Matters.

01

Growth of AI infrastructure

Expansion of AI training, inference, GPU cloud, and private AI increases the value, and the exposure, of the infrastructure behind it.

02

Patented intellectual property

Three approved patents cover adaptive detection, risk propagation, and impact-aware decision intelligence.

03

Differentiated infrastructure intelligence

As AI makes threat discovery more widely available, value shifts toward understanding consequence and deciding how to respond.

04

Potentially scalable licensing

Proposed models include platform subscriptions, infrastructure-based licensing, OEM, and channel distribution.

05

Model-agnostic architecture

The decision layer is designed to benefit from advancing AI models without depending on a single provider.

06

Clear development milestones

A staged path from architecture and IP through validation, pilots, and commercialization.
