Trust & security

Security and Governance by Design.

A platform that can act on infrastructure has to earn trust before it earns authority. CyberActive AI is designed so customers control what it can see, what it can do, and how every decision is recorded.

Discuss Security RequirementsGoverned Autonomy

Design principles

Control Stays With the Customer.

01

Customer-defined authority

Customers choose the mode for each action class and environment. Nothing executes outside the boundaries they approve.

02

Least-privilege integrations

The platform acts only through the integrations and scoped permissions a customer grants, never through unrestricted access.

03

Complete traceability

Every recommendation and action is recorded with its evidence, rationale, approver, and outcome for audit and review.

04

Human override

Authorized operators can pause, reverse, or override any action at any stage, in every mode.

05

Safe evaluation

Observe mode lets teams evaluate recommendations against real conditions without changing production systems.

06

Data minimization

The architecture is designed to use the signals and context needed for decisions, with retention defined per engagement.

Responsible disclosure

Report a Security Issue.

If you believe you have found a vulnerability in this website or in CyberActive AI technology, please tell us privately so we can investigate and fix it before it is disclosed.

  • Email security@cyberactive.ai with details and reproduction steps
  • Give us reasonable time to respond before public disclosure
  • Do not access, change, or delete data that is not yours
  • Do not degrade service or run denial-of-service tests
  • We will acknowledge reports and keep you informed
  • Machine-readable policy at /.well-known/security.txt

Statuses reflect current plans and are updated as milestones are reached. Customer security and compliance requirements are reviewed during technical evaluation.
