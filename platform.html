The market challenge

AI Infrastructure Is Scaling. Security Complexity Is Accelerating.

AI workloads depend on interconnected GPU compute, networking, storage, identity, orchestration, and cloud services. As that infrastructure grows, a single incident can reach far beyond the system where it started.

01

High-value AI assets

GPU infrastructure, models, training data, and inference services concentrate technology value and intellectual property.

02

Interconnected infrastructure

A compromise of one component can create cascading risk across compute, networking, identity, storage, and workloads.

03

Faster discovery, less time to respond

Automation, including AI-assisted tooling, can shorten the gap between discovering a weakness and exploiting it, which leaves less time for investigation and decisions.

04

Operational consequences

Every containment action affects something. Security decisions must weigh workload availability and service commitments.

Governed autonomy

Autonomy Within Boundaries You Approve.

Operators decide how much authority the platform has, for which actions, on which infrastructure. Autonomous action is limited to action classes the customer has explicitly approved, and a human can override any step.

Observe

Analyze, correlate, and recommend. The platform makes no changes to production systems.

Authority
None. Recommendations only
Human role
Operators decide and act
Typical use
Evaluation, onboarding, highly sensitive systems

Defined authorization boundaries

Customers set which action classes, assets, and environments the platform may act on, and in which mode.

Policy enforcement

Every proposed action is checked against policy before it is offered or executed.

Human approval requirements

Approval rules define who approves which actions, including two-person rules for disruptive ones.

Action traceability

Each recommendation and action is recorded with its evidence, rationale, approver, and outcome.

Operational safeguards

Blast-radius limits, protected assets, and rate limits keep responses proportionate.

Human override

Authorized operators can pause, reverse, or override any action at any stage.

Recovery verification

Restoration is checked against a known-good state before systems return to service.

Customer-approved boundaries

The platform never receives unrestricted access to customer infrastructure. It acts only through integrations and permissions the customer grants, within boundaries the customer approves.
