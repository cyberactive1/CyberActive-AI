The market challenge

AI Infrastructure Is Scaling. Security Complexity Is Accelerating.

AI workloads depend on interconnected GPU compute, networking, storage, identity, orchestration, and cloud services. As infrastructure expands, a security incident can create consequences far beyond a single compromised system. Protecting these environments requires understanding how threats move, which workloads are exposed, and how security actions may affect critical operations.

01

High-Value AI Assets

GPU infrastructure, AI models, training data, and inference services represent valuable technology and intellectual property.

02

03

Accelerating Threats

Automated attacks compress the time available for investigation, decision-making, and response.

04

Operational Consequences

Security decisions must balance containment with workload availability and service continuity.
