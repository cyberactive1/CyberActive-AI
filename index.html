The new adversary

AI has changed cyber warfare.

For decades, attacks were planned and executed by people. Today's adversaries increasingly use AI to operate autonomously — evolving faster than human-operated teams can investigate, coordinate, and respond.

Machine-speed attacks require machine-speed decisions.
