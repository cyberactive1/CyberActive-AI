The new adversary

AI has changed cyber warfare.

For decades, attacks were planned and executed by people. Today's adversaries increasingly use AI to operate autonomously — evolving faster than human-operated teams can investigate, coordinate, and respond.

Machine-speed attacks require machine-speed decisions.

Learn from failed attempts

Securing AI & GPU workloads

Your AI Is Only as Valuable as It Is Secure.

GPU clusters, models, and training data represent extraordinary concentrations of value — and extraordinary targets. CyberActive AI applies autonomous security technology to the assets that matter most in AI infrastructure, understanding each workload in the context of the compute, identity, and data it depends on.

Threats we're built for Model theft & exfiltration Data & model poisoning GPU resource hijacking Tenant breakout Control-plane compromise Inference API abuse
01

Model weights & checkpoints

Your most valuable IP. Detect anomalous access, exfiltration, and tampering of trained models and training checkpoints.

02

Training pipelines & datasets

Protect data integrity across ingestion, preprocessing, and training — where poisoning and supply-chain compromise begin.

03

Inference endpoints & APIs

Identify abuse, credential misuse, and anomalous traffic against production inference services.

04

GPU clusters & management planes

Context across GPU hosts, BMC and management interfaces, fabric, and firmware — where a single compromise has the largest blast radius.

05

Multi-tenant isolation

Understand tenant relationships and shared infrastructure so one tenant's compromise does not become another's outage.

06

Orchestration & identity

Kubernetes, schedulers, service accounts, and privileged access — the control plane attackers target to reach AI workloads.
