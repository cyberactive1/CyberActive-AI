Beyond threat detection

Finding a Threat Is Only the Beginning.

Advanced AI is transforming how vulnerabilities are discovered and attacks are analyzed. But identifying a threat is only the first step.

The greater challenge is understanding how that threat affects interconnected AI workloads, GPU infrastructure, and critical services, and determining how to respond without creating unnecessary operational disruption.

CyberActive AI is designed to bridge that gap through patented risk propagation intelligence, business-impact analysis, governed autonomous decisions, and coordinated recovery.

The CyberActive AI platform

Detect. Understand. Decide. Act. Restore.

One continuous lifecycle that complements the security investments you already have.

Stage 01 · Detect

Signals from compute, identity, network, storage, and existing security tools are evaluated continuously for behavior that departs from established operating patterns.

What happens at this stage

  • Ingests signals from existing security and infrastructure tools
  • Behavior-oriented analysis, not signatures alone
  • Designed to surface previously unseen activity

Stage 02 · Understand

Events are placed in context: which assets, identities, and services are connected, and what those relationships mean for the environment.

What happens at this stage

  • Maintains a model of asset and service relationships
  • Links identity, workload, and network context
  • Turns isolated alerts into connected situations

Stage 03 · Decide

The platform weighs how a threat could spread and what it would affect, then ranks candidate actions by risk, disruption, and policy.

What happens at this stage

  • Assesses potential propagation paths
  • Considers operational and business criticality
  • Produces explainable, policy-checked recommendations

Stage 04 · Act

Authorized actions are coordinated across integrated controls, including identity, network, and orchestration, within the permissions and safeguards operators define.

What happens at this stage

  • Observe, Approve, or Autonomous operating modes
  • Enforcement through existing, customer-owned controls
  • Every action recorded and attributable

Stage 05 · Restore

Recovery is coordinated and validated, and the outcome of each event informs how future situations are evaluated.

What happens at this stage

  • Coordinated restoration of affected services
  • Integrity and state validation before return to service
  • Outcomes feed continuous improvement

Governed autonomy

Autonomy Within Boundaries You Approve.

You decide how much authority the platform has, for which actions, on which infrastructure. A human can override any step.

01

Observe

Analyze, correlate, and recommend. The platform makes no changes to production systems.

Authority
None. Recommendations only
Human role
Operators decide and act
02

Approve

Prepare response actions for authorized human review. Nothing runs until a named approver accepts it.

Authority
Executes only after approval
Human role
Approve, modify, or reject each action
03

Autonomous

Execute pre-authorized action classes within customer-approved boundaries, then notify. Anything outside those boundaries escalates to a human.

Authority
Only action classes the customer pre-approves
Human role
Sets boundaries, is notified, can override
✓Customer-approved boundaries. The platform never receives unrestricted access to customer infrastructure. It acts only through integrations and permissions the customer grants, within boundaries the customer approves. Disruptive actions always require human approval.
